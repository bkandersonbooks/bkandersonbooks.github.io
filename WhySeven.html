About This Book

Why seven?

Across thousands of years, civilizations separated by oceans, languages, and centuries have returned again and again to the same number.

Seven stars. Seven sages. Seven heavens. Seven gates. Seven sacred days.

From prehistoric monuments and the earliest recorded civilizations to ancient religious traditions and the mysteries preserved in mythology, the number seven appears with remarkable persistence.

But why?

In WHY SEVEN? — Following an Ancient Pattern Through Time, B. K. Anderson follows the trail backward through history, exploring archaeology, astronomy, ancient texts, cultural traditions, and the developing relationship between human observation and symbolic meaning.

The journey examines the ancient Near East, Egypt, Mesopotamia, early monumental sites, sacred traditions, and the ways societies preserved, transformed, and passed knowledge from one generation to another.

Rather than assuming that every appearance of seven shares a single origin, the exploration asks which connections can be supported by historical evidence, which may reflect common human experiences, and which remain mysteries.

Could the pattern have begun with observations of the natural world? Did certain traditions spread between cultures? Or did people separated by vast distances independently find meaning in the same number?

This is not a book that asks readers to accept a predetermined answer.

It invites them to follow the evidence, consider the possibilities, and continue asking questions.

Because sometimes the most revealing discoveries begin with the simplest question:

WHY SEVEN?
