About This Book

What if the stories of our past contain more than we have realized?

A visit to Mesa Verde. A question about the Hopi. An ancient story from Sumer. One question leads to another—and soon a much larger pattern begins to emerge.

In What If?, B. K. Anderson follows an unexpected trail through archaeology, ancient history, mythology, science, and spiritual traditions. From the American Southwest to ancient Mesopotamia, the journey explores stories of lost worlds, floods and destruction, mysterious teachers, the Anunnaki, the Apkallū, Eridu, ancient cycles, and humanity's enduring search for its own beginnings.

But this is not a book that tells you what to believe.

Instead, it asks what happens when we place ancient stories beside what archaeology and science have discovered—and remain willing to follow the questions wherever they lead.

Could myths preserve distant memories of real events? Have civilizations risen, fallen, and begun again more times than we realize? Why do similar themes appear among peoples separated by enormous distances and thousands of years? And what might humanity's forgotten past tell us about ourselves today?

Some questions lead to evidence. Others lead to mysteries that remain unsolved. And sometimes, even a wrong question opens the right door.

What If? is the first book in a two-book exploration of humanity's past, the patterns that may have shaped civilization, and the possibility that understanding where we have been may change the questions we ask about where we are going.

You don't have to believe.

You only have to be willing to ask: What if?
